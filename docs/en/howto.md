1. **How to create an account?**
* Open the `.env` file and change `ADMIN_LOGIN=YOUR_LOGIN` and `ADMIN_PASSWORD=YOUR_PASSWORD`. Congratulations! You have created an account.


2. **How to log in?**
* Go to `https://your_[domain.com/login](https://domain.com/login)` and log into your account.


3. **How to create a new article/post?**
* Go to `https://your_[domain.com/admin/article/new](https://domain.com/admin/article/new)` and write your article. At the very bottom, click the **Publish** button.


4. **How to upload a file?**
* Go to `https://your_[domain.com/admin/file](https://domain.com/admin/file)` and click **Upload new file**.


5. **How to delete a file?**
* Go to `https://your_[domain.com/admin/file](https://domain.com/admin/file)` and click the **Trash can** icon.


6. **How to view the list of all files?**
* Go to `https://your_[domain.com/admin/file](https://domain.com/admin/file)` to view the list of all uploaded files. *Note: Previews are only available for images.*


7. **How to delete an article/post?**
* Go to `https://your_[domain.com/article](https://domain.com/article)`. If you are logged in, you will see a **Trash can** icon. Click it, confirm the deletion, and the article/post will be removed.


8. **How to edit an article/post?**
* Go to `https://your_[domain.com/article](https://domain.com/article)`. If you are logged in, you will see a **Pencil** icon. Click it to open the article editor page.
